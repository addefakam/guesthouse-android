Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Guesthouse Manager ("we", "us", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Username and password used to authenticate with the guesthouse management system.

Guest Information: Names, phone numbers, ID numbers, nationality, and address details of guests checked into the guesthouse. This data is entered by authorized staff.

Reservation Data: Check-in/check-out dates, room assignments, payment records, and reservation status.

Activity Logs: Staff actions (check-in, check-out, payments, cancellations) are logged with timestamp and IP address for audit purposes.

Camera Access: The app requests camera access for QR code scanning during guest check-in. No photos are stored unless explicitly captured by staff.

Device Information: IP address used for audit logging. No device identifiers are collected.

2. How We Use Information

  • To manage guesthouse operations (reservations, check-ins, payments)
  • To maintain audit trails for accountability and fraud prevention
  • To generate business reports and analytics
  • To comply with local law enforcement reporting requirements
  • To send push notifications about reservations and alerts

3. Data Storage

All data is stored on secure cloud servers (Vercel + PostgreSQL). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (database-level encryption).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Guest information may be shared with local law enforcement agencies when legally required. Aggregate (non-personal) data may be used for platform analytics.

5. Data Retention

Guest records are retained for the duration of the guesthouse's subscription. Staff activity logs are retained indefinitely for audit purposes. Camera snapshots (if enabled) are retained for 90 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact your system administrator to exercise these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact your system administrator.
