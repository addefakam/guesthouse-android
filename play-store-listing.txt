# ── Full description (4000 chars max) ──

Guesthouse Manager is a comprehensive management tool for guesthouse operators and staff. Manage your entire property from your phone or tablet — no computer required.

KEY FEATURES:

🏠 RESERVATIONS
• Create and manage reservations
• One-tap check-in and check-out
• Early checkout support
• Bulk actions (check-in/out multiple reservations)
• Payment recording (cash, transfer, card, mobile)
• QR code scanner for fast ID entry

🛏️ ROOMS
• Real-time room availability
• 10 room types (Single, Double, Twin, Suite, Deluxe, King, etc.)
• Room status tracking (Available, Occupied, Reserved, Maintenance)
• Floor and capacity management

👥 GUEST MANAGEMENT
• Guest database with search
• National ID support (Ethiopian Fayda ID)
• Guest history and stay tracking
• VIP guest flagging
• Nationality tracking (197 countries)

📦 STOCK MANAGEMENT
• Inventory tracking with predefined items
• Restock with automatic expense recording
• Stock movement audit log
• Low-stock alerts
• Category management (Linen, Toiletries, Beverage, etc.)

💰 EXPENSES
• Track all business expenses
• Categorize with colors and icons
• Payment method tracking
• Tax amount recording
• Auto-generated expenses from stock restocks

📊 REPORTS
• Revenue and expense tracking
• Occupancy rate analytics
• Daily revenue charts
• Expense breakdown by category
• Export to CSV/Excel

🔐 SECURITY
• Staff activity logging (who did what, when, from which IP)
• Role-based access control
• Operator and staff permission management
• Secure authentication

LANGUAGES:
• English
• አማርኛ (Amharic)
• Afaan Oromoo (Oromo)

WHO IS THIS FOR?
• Guesthouse owners managing properties remotely
• Front-desk staff handling day-to-day operations
• Guesthouse chains with multiple properties
• Hotels, lodges, and resorts in Ethiopia

PRIVACY:
• All data is securely stored and encrypted
• Staff actions are logged for accountability
• No personal data is shared with third parties
• See our privacy policy for details
